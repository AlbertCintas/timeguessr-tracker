About Timeguessr Club

Timeguessr Club is a free, independent scoreboard for a group of friends who play Timeguessr. It keeps their game results together so they can compare scores over time.

This project is maintained by AlbertCintas. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Timeguessr. To play the original game, visit timeguessr.com.

How the standings work

  • Points adds each player’s submitted scores. The per-game option shows their average score instead.
  • Wins counts games where a player has the highest submitted score. Tied highest scores each count as a win. The per-game option shows the percentage of their recorded games won.
  • Standings include all recorded games by default. Period filters let you compare results within a selected week, month, year or other available period.
  • Picture records compare submitted picture scores, year errors and distance errors. These are statistics from the guessing game.

Rankings reflect the results recorded here. New submissions, corrections or deletions can change them; this is a friends’ tracker, not an official Timeguessr competition.

Accounts and adding results

Anyone can view the scoreboard. Adding results requires an invitation from the club administrator and a separate tracker username and password. A Timeguessr account does not provide access to this tracker.

Signed-in players can paste Timeguessr’s shared results, enter scores manually, or use the companion browser extension. Detailed shared results include the five picture scores and their year and distance errors.

The companion extension

The Chrome, Edge and Firefox companion extension reads completed Timeguessr results and imports them into this scoreboard for the signed-in tracker account. Keep the completed results page open until the extension captures it. Its popup shows import status and lets you pause imports, retry pending uploads or sign out.

The extension is designed for this club’s scoreboard. It does not sync a Timeguessr account or require your Timeguessr password. Conflicting existing scores require manual review rather than being overwritten automatically.

Privacy and public results

Usernames, display names, uploaded avatars, scores and picture statistics are publicly visible. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; Supabase provides authentication and stores the club’s data. There are no ads or analytics trackers.

Read the privacy policy for details about information collected, local extension storage and deletion requests.

Source code and support

The source code is available on GitHub. Report technical problems through the issue tracker. For invitations or account help, contact the club administrator privately. Do not post passwords or private account details in public issues.
